## Local-only provenance

`.pio`, generated firmware, `.vscode`/assistant state, ignored logs, local `docs/archive`, preservation receipts, original video and ZIP evidence are excluded from the source package. Existing references to them in historical records are provenance pointers. They remain preserved locally; the release does not claim to redistribute every raw experimental recording.

## Review method

All delivered paths were classified; Markdown headings/local targets were inventoried; current entrypoints were reconciled against source and dated records. Original OOXML archives and image decodes were checked without resaving. Source/configuration and historical assertion/evidence preservation were verified by byte hashes. This review is repository/software QA, not independent authentication of every physical observation or a new experimental result.
