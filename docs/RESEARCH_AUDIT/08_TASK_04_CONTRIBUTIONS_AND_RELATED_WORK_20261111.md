# PIKU 2.1 Rev A scientific contribution and related work audit

Checkpoint: 11 November 2026, Asia/Dhaka. Baseline: local `master`, `ceb3b078bcdff084addb29f9fda0f6d615779624`. This record preserves the completed 12-paper Task 4 review and checks it against Task 1–3 records and current source. It documents recommendations for supervisor review; it does not edit the paper or establish conference acceptance.

**Finding:** PIKU has a defensible engineering contribution in integrating freshness-aware sensing, finite recovery attempts, operator authority and environmental monitoring on an approximately BDT 5,000 ESP32-S3 prototype. The individual techniques have substantial prior work. The available experiments characterize this implementation; they do not establish a new general navigation algorithm, first-ever integration, causal superiority or certified safety.

## Evidence and review levels

- **IMPLEMENTED:** checked in local firmware, not automatically demonstrated in every physical trial.
- **OWNER-REPORTED PHYSICAL:** supplied observations or manual measurements; this audit did not operate the robot or independently replay all trials.
- **CALCULATED:** arithmetic from documented inputs; not an additional measurement.
- **PRIOR TECHNIQUE:** a method already represented in the literature below.
- **UNVERIFIED:** a conclusion or identity link not established by the inspected records.

Literature levels retain the completed review's scope: **F = FULL-TEXT REVIEWED**, with relevant methods/evaluation/limitations inspected; this does not claim exhaustive reading of every survey page. **A = ABSTRACT-ONLY**, sometimes with publisher previews or an indexed first page; no claims depending on inaccessible methods are made. Six works are F and six are A. **METADATA-ONLY** verification establishes a citation's identity, not its findings. A failed link refresh at this checkpoint does not retroactively change the earlier review level. **NR** below means not established from the reviewed material; it never means that a feature is absent. This is a focused review, not an exhaustive systematic search or proof of firstness.

## Four narrow contribution statements for Task 5

These are proposed wording, qualified by the evidence above and subject to the supervisor's manuscript review.

1. **Integrated platform:** “We present an approximately BDT 5,000 ESP32-S3 mobile prototype that combines reactive navigation, environmental indication and browser-based operator control in a standalone embedded system.”
2. **Evidence-aware control:** “The controller checks sensor validity, sample age and scan association before applicable autonomous actions, and uses checked IMU samples for gyro-assisted turns.”
3. **Coordinated recovery:** “A committed-side recovery policy limits each attempt's turns, backoff actions and elapsed duration while coordinating operator STOP, mode authority and applicable environmental guards.”
4. **Physical characterization:** “We report a 20-trial pilot, two separate corridor-escape experiments and 40 manual turn-angle measurements, distinguishing goal attainment, contact events and nominal-target angle error.”

These describe engineering implementation and bounded observations. They must not become claims of a first-ever method, new AI, learned mapping, SLAM, industrial gas concentration accuracy, measured millisecond response, collision-free performance, certified safety, guaranteed escape, or controlled superiority over prior robots. The literature supports the importance of the problem and the existence of prior approaches; it does not validate untested PIKU benefits.
